# Job Cards - Mechanic Tasks

Define work assignment, task updates, and completion notes for technicians.

---

## Findings during inspections

On the job card there is a **Findings during inspections** section. Use it to record **technical problems, defects, or any issues** discovered on the vehicle while the car is in the workshop—not only the work for this visit, but anything worth remembering for **future service**.

Advisors and mechanics can review these findings on a **later visit** so recurring conditions, deferred work, or prior observations are not lost between jobs.

---

## Task details and multi-stage troubleshooting

In **job card task details**, work can be broken into **multiple troubleshooting stages** (for example diagnose → repair → verify). For **each stage** you can capture:

- **Effort** — what was done in that stage  
- **Parts used** — components consumed or fitted in that stage  
- **Time taken** — labour or elapsed time for that stage  

This gives a clear history of how the fix was approached step by step, supports billing and warranty notes, and helps the next technician understand what was already tried.

---

## Outsource a particular task

From a **job card task**, you can **outsource that specific task** when the work will be done by an external workshop instead of in-house. The task stays on the job card so everyone sees that the line is external, while costing and status follow the outsource workflow.

**Vendor choice** comes from **Outsourced Services**: only **vendors that have services added** (specialties and offerings configured on the vendor record) appear where they match the kind of work you are sending out. That keeps the picker aligned with who can actually perform the task.

Starting the outsource action from the task ties into **outsource requests** (what was sent, expected cost, and progress). For vendor lists, add/edit vendor, and request status, see [Outsourced services](../accounts/outsource-services.md).
